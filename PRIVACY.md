# ByeDirty privacy policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

ByeDirty is an Android app that reads filter and shower data from a compatible smart shower head over NFC.
It's an independent project by Alexander Troshchenko and isn't affiliated with the shower head's maker.

## What ByeDirty collects

Nothing. ByeDirty has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no internet access (the app doesn't request the
internet permission).

## What stays on your phone

When you hold your phone to the shower head, ByeDirty reads the tag's memory: filter usage, settings and a log of
recent showers (length, water used, time with hot or cold water). It stores what it reads, your showers-per-week
setting and your reminder preference in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is sent anywhere.

Android's own backup may include this storage in your device backup if you have backup turned on. That backup is
handled by Google under your Google account, not by ByeDirty.

Scans you choose to share from the info screen (**Collect data → Share**) go wherever you send them. ByeDirty
only hands the files to Android's share sheet.

## Permissions

- **NFC:** to read the shower head.
- **Notifications:** to remind you when the filter is due, only if you turn the reminder on.
- **Run at startup:** to put the reminder back after your phone restarts.

## Deleting your data

Clear ByeDirty's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything it saved.
